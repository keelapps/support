# Support

This is where bugs, feature requests, and questions about keelapps products are
tracked. The products themselves are closed-source; this repository is the
public record of what has been reported, what is being worked on, and what has
been fixed.

## Reporting something

[Open an issue](https://github.com/keelapps/support/issues/new/choose) and pick
the template that fits — a bug report, a feature request, or a question,
including questions from before you install anything.

Every template asks which product you mean. Please answer it; that is what
routes the issue. Bug reports are additionally split by platform, because the
details that identify a problem in an Atlassian Cloud app and a problem in a
Mac app have nothing in common.

## What does not belong here

This tracker is public and permanent. Two things should go elsewhere:

- **Security vulnerabilities** — report them privately. See [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
- **Billing, licensing, refunds, and evaluation periods** — for anything bought
  through the Atlassian Marketplace these are handled by Atlassian, not by us.
  Contact [Atlassian Marketplace support](https://support.atlassian.com/contact/).
  For products we sell directly, use the support address on that product's own
  site.

## What to expect

We read everything. Issues are triaged within a few working days, and we would
rather tell you plainly that something is out of scope than leave it open
indefinitely. Issues closed without a fix will say why.

## Legal

Apps sold through the Atlassian Marketplace adopt the Atlassian standard
end-user agreement (Bonterms v1.0) and publish a per-app privacy policy. They
are zero-egress: they run on Atlassian Forge, store data in the customer's own
Atlassian tenant, and the vendor never touches customer data — so we are not a
data processor under GDPR and do not sign separate DPAs.

Products we sell directly are not covered by any of that. Each publishes its
own end-user agreement and privacy policy on its own site.
