# Support

> **Support has moved.** Bugs, feature requests, and questions now go to the
> [Keelapps support portal](https://keelapps.atlassian.net/helpcenter/support/),
> or by email to [support@keelapps.atlassian.net](mailto:support@keelapps.atlassian.net). No GitHub
> account needed, and your request is private to you and us.

This repository remains the public home of our
[security policy](SECURITY.md) and the archive of previously reported issues.


## Reporting something

Raise it at the [Keelapps support portal](https://keelapps.atlassian.net/helpcenter/support/) —
bug reports, feature requests, and questions, including questions from before
you install anything. Say which app you mean; that is what routes the request.

## What does not belong there

Two things should go elsewhere:

- **Security vulnerabilities** — report them privately. See [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
- **Billing, licensing, refunds, and evaluation periods** — for anything bought
  through the Atlassian Marketplace these are handled by Atlassian, not by us.
  Contact [Atlassian Marketplace support](https://support.atlassian.com/contact/).

## What to expect

We read everything. Issues are triaged within a few working days, and we would
rather tell you plainly that something is out of scope than leave it open
indefinitely. Issues closed without a fix will say why.

## Legal

Apps sold through the Atlassian Marketplace adopt the Atlassian standard
end-user agreement (Bonterms v1.0) and publish a per-app privacy policy. They
are zero-egress: they run on Atlassian Forge, store data in the customer's own
Atlassian tenant, and the vendor never touches customer data — so we are not a
data processor under GDPR and do not sign separate DPAs.
